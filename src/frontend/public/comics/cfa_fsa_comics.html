CFA Level I 2025 Comic Book

Financial Statement Analysis

Turn financial statements into an analyst narrative. Built from the L1 2025 subject outline and rewritten as fast visual revision panels.

SubjectFinancial Statement Analysis
Panels11 learning modules
UseQuick revision before practice
01

Introduction to FSA

BAM

The analyst starts with a question, then gathers, cleans, interprets, and reports evidence.

Exam lens: Use the analysis framework and read filings, notes, MD&A, auditor reports, and segments.
02

Income Statements

ZOOM

Revenue and expenses enter the scene, but timing rules decide the panel order.

Exam lens: Watch recognition, capitalization versus expensing, nonrecurring items, and margin trends.
03

Balance Sheets

AHA

Assets, liabilities, and equity freeze the company at one reporting date.

Exam lens: Liquidity, solvency, working capital, and accounting choices shape interpretation.
04

Cash Flow Statements I

POW

Cash tells a less polished story than accrual income.

Exam lens: Separate operating, investing, and financing flows; compare direct and indirect methods.
05

Cash Flow Statements II

FOCUS

The analyst rebuilds free cash flow from the cash-flow panels.

Exam lens: FCFF, FCFE, coverage, and cash conversion reveal durability.
06

Inventories

CHECK

Inventory accounting can move profit without moving products.

Exam lens: FIFO, weighted average, write-downs, turnover, and gross margin need careful comparison.
07

Long-Term Assets

BAM

Big assets create depreciation, impairment, and revaluation storylines.

Exam lens: Capitalization choices affect income, assets, ratios, and future expenses.
08

Long-Term Liabilities and Equity

ZOOM

Debt, leases, pensions, and equity accounts change both risk and claims.

Exam lens: Look for covenants, leverage, book value changes, and off-balance-sheet pressure.
09

Income Taxes

AHA

Accounting profit and taxable profit rarely walk in perfect sync.

Exam lens: Deferred tax assets/liabilities come from temporary differences and future expectations.
10

Reporting Quality

POW

Numbers can be useful, biased, aggressive, or outright low quality.

Exam lens: Assess sustainability, estimates, earnings management signals, and restatements.
11

Financial Analysis Techniques

FOCUS

Ratios become useful when compared across time, peers, and strategy.

Exam lens: Common-size, trend, activity, liquidity, solvency, profitability, and DuPont tools connect.

Last-page checklist

After reading this comic, close the page and explain each panel aloud in one sentence. If a panel feels fuzzy, open that module in your CFA notes and do 5 practice questions before moving on.
