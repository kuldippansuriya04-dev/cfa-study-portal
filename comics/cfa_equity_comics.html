The financial system exists to move money where it's NEEDED most!

Secondary → Subsequent trades

Order Types
Execute the Trade!
Market Order Execute immediately at best price

Limit Order Avoid price uncertainty. Might not fill!

Stop Loss Triggered at set price to protect position
Leverage
Margin Math!
Leverage = 1/IM
Margin Call = S₀ × (1−IM)/(1−MM)
If S₀=100, IM=40%, MM=20%:
Leverage = 2.5 · Call price = 75
Market Types
Quote-Driven vs Order-Driven vs Brokered!
Quote Driven
Dealers post bid/ask. Maintain inventory. Also called OTC / dealer markets.
Order Driven
Orders matched by trading rules. Exchanges & automated trading systems.
Brokered
Broker finds the counterparty. Used for unique/illiquid assets.

Leverage Trio
DOL, DFL, DCL!
DOL = % ∆EBIT / % ∆Sales = (Sales−VC)/EBIT
DFL = % ∆EPS / % ∆EBIT = EBIT/EBT
DCL = DOL × DFL = % ∆EPS / % ∆Sales
High fixed costs → High DOL → Bigger profit swings!

💎 Chapter 8: Equity Valuation: The Money Chapter!
3 Model Types
Valuation Arsenal!
DCF Model PV of future cash flows

Multiplier Model P/E, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA

Asset-Based MV Assets − MV Liabilities

Fairly valued: Market = Intrinsic
Undervalued: Market < Intrinsic ✅
Overvalued: Market > Intrinsic ❌
DDM Formula
Dividend Discount Model!
V₀ = D₁ / (Kₑ − g)
Gordon Growth Model assumes:
ROE is constant · Payout ratio constant · g = ROE × Retention ratio

g↑ → V↑ · Kₑ↑ → V↓ · D↑ → V↑

Dividend displacement: D↑ → retention↓ → g↓ → V↓
Enterprise Value
Total Company Worth!
EV = MV Equity + MV Debt + MV Preferred − Cash − ST Investments
Best when firms have very different capital structures.

Most popular denominator: EBITDA

EV/EBITDA is widely used for comparisons!
DDM Stages
Multi-Stage Models!
1Constant Growth DDM — Stable/mature firm, constant dividend growth forever
22-Stage DDM — High growth now, then drops to stable growth. Temporary growth phase!
33-Stage DDM — Young firm still in high growth phase. Three distinct periods.
Multiplier Models
Price Ratios!
P/E = MPS/EPS
P/B = MPS/BVPS
P/S = MPS/Sales per share
P/CF = MPS/CF per share
Justified (fundamental) P/E = Payout ratio / (Kₑ − g)
Model Showdown
Advantages vs Disadvantages!
DCF / PV Model
✅ Easy to calculate, widely accepted
❌ Sensitive to estimates, inputs must be estimated
Multiplier Model
✅ Widely used, useful for cross-sectional comparisons
❌ Negative denominator problem, not comparable internationally
Asset-Based Model
✅ Provides floor values
❌ MVs hard to get, inaccurate with intangibles, hard in hyperinflation
