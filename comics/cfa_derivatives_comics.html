💥 Chapter 1: What IS a Derivative?!
The Big Definition
A derivative DERIVES its value from an underlying asset — stocks, bonds, currencies, commodities!
EExchange-Traded (ETD) — Standardized, backed by clearinghouse. Eg. Options & Futures
OOver-the-Counter (OTC) — Custom contracts, dealer market, NO central location. Eg. Forwards, Swaps
OTC vs ETD Showdown!
Two Worlds of Derivatives!
OTC
Custom · Illiquid · Default risk · Unregulated · Flexible
ETD
Standard · Liquid · Clearinghouse · Regulated · Transparent
Value at initiation = ZERO for both!
Underlyings
What's underneath?
Equities Stocks & indexes
Fixed Income Bonds, rates
Currencies FX rates
Commodities Gold, oil, wheat
Credit CDS, default risk
Other Weather, crypto
Forward Commitments
Must perform!
Legally binding promise to act in the future.

Forwards OTC customized
Futures Exchange-traded
Swaps Series of payments

Both parties OBLIGATED to perform!
Contingent Claims
Only if triggered!
Claim that depends on a particular event occurring.

Options Right but NOT obligation
Credit Derivatives CDS

One party has a CHOICE — that's what makes them special!
📜 Chapter 2: Forwards vs Futures — The Epic Battle!
OTC Custom!
Forwards
Private Contracts!
→Buyer (long) agrees to buy, seller (short) agrees to sell at set price on set date
→No payment at initiation
→Customized · Illiquid · Default risk
→Not regulated · Not exchange-traded
Exchange Traded!
Futures
Standardized!
→Standardized forward contracts on an exchange
→Require margin deposit (initial margin)
→Liquid · Backed by clearinghouse
→Daily cash settlement (mark-to-market!)
→Regulated · Traded in secondary market
Margin Mechanics
Futures Margin System!
Initial Margin
Deposited to open a futures account. Security deposit required upfront.
Maintenance Margin
Minimum balance that must be maintained. Falls below? Margin call!
Variation Margin
Equity account: top up to maintenance margin. Futures account: top up to INITIAL margin!
🎯 Chapter 2 Continued: Options — The Right, Not the Obligation!
Call Options
The RIGHT to BUY!
Long Call
Right to BUY · Pays premium
Max profit: INFINITE!
Max loss: Premium paid
Short Call
Obligation to SELL · Receives premium
Max profit: Premium
Max loss: INFINITE!
Breakeven for call = X + P
Put Options
The RIGHT to SELL!
Long Put
Right to SELL · Pays premium
Max profit: X − P
Max loss: Premium paid
Short Put
Obligation to BUY · Receives premium
Max profit: Premium
Max loss: X − P
Breakeven for put = X − P
Option Styles
When to exercise?
American Exercise ANY time before expiry

European Only ON expiry date

Bermudan Only on certain dates (eg. once/month)

At expiration, American = European!
MONEYNESS!
Call option:
ITM: S > X · ATM: S = X · OTM: S < X

Put option:
ITM: X > S · ATM: X = S · OTM: X < S

Option Premium = Intrinsic Value + Time Value
Option Value
Exercise Values!
Call: max(0, St − FV(X))
Put: max(0, FV(X) − St)

Intrinsic value is NEVER negative!

Time value can be negative for deep ITM European puts.
🔄 Chapter 2 Continued: Swaps — The Exchange Game!
Plain Vanilla Swap
Fixed for Floating!
Exchange a series of payments on periodic settlement dates. Payments are NETTED — only one payment made each period.

Eg. Fixed 8% vs LIBOR + 2%, Notional = 100,000:
LIBOR=6%
Net = 0%
No payment
LIBOR=9%
Net = +3%
A receives 3000
LIBOR=4%
Net = −2%
A pays 2000
Swap Key Facts
Must Know!
→No payment at initiation (except currency swaps)
→Custom OTC instruments — not exchange-traded
→Largely unregulated with default risk
→Length of swap = tenor
→Only large institutions participate

⚡ Chapter 3: Benefits & Risks of Derivatives!
Derivative BENEFITS
Why use them?!
1Risk Management — Transfer exposure without trading the asset directly
2Information Discovery — Reveal future prices and risk expectations
3Operational Efficiency — Lower transaction costs, enable short selling
4Market Efficiency — Faster price reflection, fewer arbitrage opportunities
Derivative RISKS
Dark side!
⚠Implicit Leverage — High leverage increases distress risk
⚠Basis Risk — Values may diverge from underlying
⚠Liquidity Risk — Cash flow mismatches
⚠Counterparty Risk — OTC default exposure
⚠Systemic Risk — Contagion effect (2008!)
💰 Chapter 4: Arbitrage — Riskless Profit!
The Golden Rule
Law of One Price!
Arbitrage = Riskless profit with no capital!

Two portfolios with identical future cash flows MUST have the same price today.

S + P = B + C
Stock + Put = Bond + Call
Protective Put = Fiduciary Call
Cost of Carry
Pricing Formula!
F₀(T) = S₀ × (1 + r)ᵀ

Costs of owning asset:
StorageInsuranceOpportunity cost

Benefits of owning asset:
DividendsInterestConvenience yield

With carry costs: F₀(T) = (S₀ − PV(I) + PV(C)) × (1+r)ᵀ
📐 Chapters 5–6: Pricing Forwards & Futures!
Initial Valuation
At Time Zero!
Forward Price = S₀(1+r)ᵀ
Initial value = ZERO

Price of forward ≠ Value of forward

Both long and short start with zero value at initiation!
During Contract Life
MTM Value!
Vt(T) = St − F₀(T)(1+r)^−(T−t)
Long position gains when spot rises above forward price.

Settlement amount at maturity (long):
VT(T) = ST − F₀(T)
FX Forward
Currency Pricing!
F₀ = S₀ × e^(rf − rd)T
Foreign rate (rf) minus domestic rate (rd).

Higher foreign rate → Higher forward price

MTM: St,f/d − F₀ × e^−(rf−rd)(T−t)
🎰 Chapters 8–9: Options Pricing & Put-Call Parity!
Factors Affecting Option Price
What moves premiums?!
↑ Call value when:
S↑ · X↓ · Time↑ · Volatility↑ · RFR↑ · Dividends↓
↑ Put value when:
S↓ · X↑ · Time↑ · Volatility↑ · RFR↓ · Dividends↑
Volatility ALWAYS increases both call and put values!
Put-Call Parity
The Master Formula!
S + P = B + C
Works ONLY for European options!

Synthetic equivalents:
S = B + C − P · P = B + C − S
C = S + P − B · B = S + P − C

+ve sign = long position   −ve sign = short position
Synthetic Positions
Build Anything from Anything!
Synthetic Call = S + P − B
If RFR ↑ → Bond ↓ → Call ↑
Synthetic Put = B + C − S
If RFR ↑ → Bond ↓ → Put ↓
🌳 Chapter 10: The Binomial Model — One Period!
The Setup
Up or Down!
Eg. S=100, X=80, Uptick=1.25, Downtick=0.8, RFR=10%

Risk-Neutral Probability (π):
π = (1+RFR − D) / (U − D)
(1.1 − 0.8) / (1.25 − 0.8) = 66.67%

Su = 125 → IV = 45    Sd = 80 → IV = 0
Option Valuation
Expected Value!
Option Value = (π × Cu + (1−π) × Cd) / (1+RFR)
= (0.667 × 45 + 0.333 × 0) / 1.1
= 30 / 1.1 = 27.27

Hedge Ratio:
h = (Cu − Cd) / (Su − Sd) = 45/45 = 1

American vs European: Equal value UNLESS early exercise has value (deep ITM put or dividend-paying call)!
