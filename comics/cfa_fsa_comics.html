💰 Chapter 4: Cash Flow Superpowers!
2 Methods!
Direct vs Indirect
CFO Two Ways!
Direct Method: Shows actual cash receipts & payments. Starts with cash from customers.

Indirect Method: Starts with Net Income, then adjusts for non-cash items.

Add back D&A ±Working Capital
IFRS vs US GAAP
Classification Battles!
Interest Received: CFO or CFI (IFRS) | CFO only (GAAP)

Interest Paid: CFO or CFF (IFRS) | CFO only (GAAP)

Dividends Paid: CFF or CFO (IFRS) | CFF only (GAAP)

Dividends Received: CFO or CFI (IFRS) | CFO only (GAAP)
📦 Chapter 5: Inventory Showdown!
COGS vs Inventory
The Cost Flow Fight!
Beg. Inv. + Purchases − COGS = End. Inv.
FIFO Old costs → COGS. New costs → Inventory.

LIFO New costs → COGS. Old costs → Inventory. (US GAAP only!)

Avg Cost Weighted average for both.
LIFO RESERVE!
LIFO Reserve = FIFO Inventory − LIFO Inventory

To convert LIFO → FIFO:
Add LIFO reserve to inventory value.

In rising prices: FIFO gives higher profits, higher taxes!
🏗️ Chapter 6: Long-Term Assets!
Depreciation
Assets Wear Out!
Straight-Line Declining Balance Units of Production

Carrying Value = Cost − Accum. Depreciation
Intangibles
You Can't Touch This!
Finite life → Amortize
Indefinite life → Annual impairment test (no amortization)

Goodwill = Price paid − Fair value of net assets. NEVER amortized!
Impairment
Asset Loses Value!
If Carrying Value > Recoverable Amount → Write down!

IFRS: Can reverse impairment losses (except goodwill)

US GAAP: Cannot reverse impairment losses!
💡 Chapter 7: Income Taxes Explained!
Two Tax Worlds
Taxable ≠ Pretax Income!
Tax Expense = Current tax + Change in deferred tax

Deferred Tax Asset — Pay taxes now, deduct later. (Prepaid taxes)

Deferred Tax Liability — Deduct now, pay taxes later. (Tax payable)
Temporary vs Permanent
Differences!
Temporary: Reverse in future → Create Deferred Tax Assets/Liabilities

Permanent: Never reverse → No deferred tax effect. Eg. Tax-exempt interest, fines.
