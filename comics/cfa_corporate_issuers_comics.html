📖 LM4: Working Capital & Liquidity — "Cash Kahan Gaya Bhai?"
Cash Conversion Cycle — The Money Pipeline
Maruti buys steel → makes car → sells → collects cash
Day 0 Buy inventory → Day 30 Sell (AR created) → Day 70 Cash received
Operating Cycle = Days Inventory (30) + Days AR (40) = 70 days
CCC = Operating Cycle (70) − Days Payable (50) = 20 days
Lower CCC = better. Amazon ka CCC negative hai bc they collect cash before paying suppliers. LEGEND! 🔥
Liquidity Ratios
Current Ratio = CA / CL
Quick Ratio = (CA − Inventory) / CL
Cash Ratio = (Cash + Mkt Sec) / CL
Activity Ratios (higher = better efficiency)
ARTR = Credit Sales / Avg AR
ITR = COGS / Avg Inventory
APTR = Purchases / Avg AP (lower = better!)

Kd = YTM × (1−t) · Kps = Annual Div / Price

Ke (Gordon) = D₁/P₀ + g
Modigliani-Miller (MM) — The Two-Proposition God Duo
MM Prop I (No Tax): Capital structure doesn't matter. VL = Vu. Firm value same regardless of debt/equity mix!
MM Prop I (With Tax): More debt = more tax shield = higher firm value! VL = Vu + (t × d)
MM Prop II (No Tax): Ke increases linearly as debt increases. Ke = Ko + D/E(Ko−Kd)
MM Prop II (With Tax): WACC minimized at 100% debt (theory!). Ke = Ko + D/E(Ko−Kd)(1−t)
MM Assumptions: No taxes, no bankruptcy costs, same expectations, borrow at RFR, no agency costs. Pure fantasy land! 😂
Trade-Off Theory vs Pecking Order vs Free CF Hypothesis
Trade-Off Theory Optimal D/E = Tax shield benefit vs cost of financial distress. Beyond optimal → value drops!
Pecking Order Theory Management prefers: Internal funds → Private debt → Public equity (last resort). Issuing equity = signal that stock is OVERVALUED 😬
Free CF Hypothesis (Jensen) More debt disciplines mgmt — forces them to manage cash efficiently. No excessive free CF!
