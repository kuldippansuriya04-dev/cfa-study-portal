1 THE FIRM & MARKET STRUCTURES
SCENE: THE PERFECT MARKET ARENA
🏪🏪🏪
Hundreds of tiny shops
We're all selling the SAME thing at the SAME price!
THE PERFECT COMPETITOR
Perfect Competition Rules:
  • Many small firms
  • Homogeneous products
  • Free entry & exit
  • Price = AR = MR
VS. THE MONOPOLIST!
🦹
I AM the market! I set the PRICE!
MR < AR... but profits are MINE!

PROFIT MAXIMIZATION
MC = MR!
The Golden Rule
Produce where Marginal Cost = Marginal Revenue. That's where profits are MAXIMIZED!
COST HEROES
Cost Squad:
  • TFC — fixed, never moves
  • TVC — rises with output
  • TC = TFC + TVC
  • AVC & ATC are U-shaped!
  • MC crosses both at minimum
MARKET STRUCTURE MAP

2 THE BUSINESS CYCLE SAGA
THE CYCLE BEGINS...
What IS a Business Cycle?
Economies go UP... and then they go DOWN. Then up again! It's the eternal cycle!
  • Recurrent but NOT periodic
  • Lasts 1–12 years
  • Affects ALL sectors simultaneously
THE 4 PHASES OF DOOM (AND BOOM!)
🌱 Recovery
→
🚀 Expansion
→
⚠️ Slowdown
→
💥 Contraction
→ 🔄
🌱 RECOVERY PHASE
📉→📈
  • Output gap narrows
  • Activity below potential
  • Inflation remains low
  • Equity markets LEAD by 3–6 months!
Buy risky assets NOW before everyone else!
🚀 EXPANSION PHASE
💰🎉
  • Boom! Strong confidence
  • Firms can't find workers
  • Wages rise sharply
  • Credit flows freely
  • Inflation picks up modestly
BOOM!
💥 CONTRACTION
📉😱
  • Layoffs begin
  • Credit tightens
  • Investors flee to SAFETY
  • Government bonds in demand
  • Inflation decelerates (with lag)
CRASH!
CREDIT CYCLE VILLAIN!
💳
Easy credit = asset bubbles! Tight credit = deeper recessions!
Latin America '80s, Asia '97, Global Financial Crisis 2008 — all had LOOSE CREDIT roots!

3&4 FISCAL & MONETARY POLICY — THE TWIN HEROES
🏛️ FISCAL POLICY — THE GOVERNMENT'S WEAPON
🏛️💰
Tools of Fiscal Policy:
  • Government SPENDING ↑↓
  • Tax rates ↑↓
  • Expansionary = spend more, tax less
  • Contractionary = spend less, tax more
Recession? Time to INJECT money into the economy!
🏦 MONETARY POLICY — THE CENTRAL BANK
🏦🔧
Tools of Monetary Policy:
  • Interest rates ↑↓
  • Open market operations
  • Reserve requirements
  • Quantitative easing (QE)
Inflation too high? RAISE rates to cool things down!
EXPANSIONARY ATTACK!
STIMULUS!
  • ↑ Gov spending
  • ↓ Taxes
  • ↓ Interest rates
  • GDP goes UP ↑
Used during RECESSIONS to boost demand!
THE MULTIPLIER EFFECT
Gov spends $1B
↓
Workers get paid
↓
Workers spend it
↓
GDP grows by MORE!
CONTRACTIONARY DEFENSE!
TIGHTEN!
  • ↓ Gov spending
  • ↑ Taxes
  • ↑ Interest rates
  • Inflation cools ↓
Used when economy is OVERHEATING!

7&8 CAPITAL FLOWS & THE FX MARKET — CURRENCY WARS!
💱 THE FOREIGN EXCHANGE ARENA
💵💶💴
The FX market is the LARGEST financial market in the world — $7+ trillion per day!
Exchange rate = price of one currency in another
EXCHANGE RATE QUOTES
Direct vs. Indirect
Direct: Domestic currency per 1 foreign unit
e.g. ₹83 / $1 USD (Indian view)

Indirect: Foreign currency per 1 domestic unit
e.g. $0.012 / ₹1
BID / ASK SPREAD!
CURRENCY APPRECIATION!
📈💪
  • Higher interest rates → capital inflow
  • Strong economy → demand for $
  • Exports become MORE expensive
  • Imports become CHEAPER
CURRENCY DEPRECIATION!
📉😰
  • Lower rates → capital outflow
  • Weak economy → sell currency
  • Exports become CHEAPER
  • Imports become MORE expensive
CROSS RATE CALCULATION!
Cross Rate Formula
EUR/JPY = EUR/USD × USD/JPY

Example:
EUR/USD = 1.10
USD/JPY = 150
EUR/JPY = 165! 🎯
PURCHASING POWER PARITY (PPP)
The Big Mac Theory!
If a Big Mac costs $5 in the US and ₹400 in India, then the "fair" rate should be ₹80/$!
Long run: exchange rates adjust so identical goods cost the same everywhere
INTEREST RATE PARITY
Covered vs Uncovered
Covered IRP: Use forward contracts to lock in FX rate — no arbitrage possible

Uncovered IRP: Expected exchange rate change = interest rate differential
