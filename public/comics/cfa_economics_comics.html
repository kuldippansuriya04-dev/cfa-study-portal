Economics

Markets, policy, trade, and FX as one connected story. Built from the L1 2025 subject outline and rewritten as fast visual revision panels.

UseQuick revision before practice
01

The Firm and Market Structures

BAM

A company captain chooses output where marginal benefit and marginal cost stop arguing.

Exam lens: Know breakeven, shutdown, scale effects, and how perfect competition differs from monopoly-style pricing.
02

Understanding Business Cycles

ZOOM

The economy moves like a dashboard with leading, coincident, and lagging lights.

Exam lens: Expansion, peak, contraction, trough, credit cycles, and investor behavior all tell timing stories.
03

Fiscal Policy

AHA

Government steps onto the stage with taxes, spending, deficits, and debt.

Exam lens: Automatic stabilizers are quiet helpers; discretionary policy is the big lever.
04

Monetary Policy

POW

The central bank adjusts money conditions while inflation and employment stare back.

Exam lens: Policy rates, transmission channels, and credibility drive market expectations.
05

Introduction to Geopolitics

FOCUS

Politics redraws the map before cash flows even reach the model.

Exam lens: Watch country risk, supply chains, sanctions, institutions, and conflict risk.
06

International Trade

CHECK

Two countries trade when specialization makes the total pile bigger.

Exam lens: Tariffs, quotas, comparative advantage, and trade barriers change winners and losers.
07

Capital Flows and the FX Market

BAM

Money crosses borders chasing return, safety, and purchasing power.

Exam lens: Balance of payments, exchange-rate regimes, and capital controls explain pressure points.
08

Exchange Rate Calculations

ZOOM

The FX desk flips quotes, cross rates, and premiums without blinking.

Exam lens: Base/price currency, bid-ask spreads, forward points, and arbitrage checks matter.
