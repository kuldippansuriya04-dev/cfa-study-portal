Derivatives

Panels10 learning modules
UseQuick revision before practice
01

Derivative Market Features

BAM

The contract gets value from something else, so the underlying is always in the frame.

Exam lens: Exchange versus OTC, clearing, margin, settlement, and counterparty risk set the stage.
02

Forward Commitments and Claims

ZOOM

Forwards, futures, and swaps lock in obligations; options create rights.

Exam lens: Distinguish payoff symmetry, settlement, and who must perform.
03

Uses, Benefits, and Risks

AHA

Derivatives can hedge, speculate, transform exposure, or create leverage.

Exam lens: The same tool can reduce risk or magnify it depending on position design.
04

Arbitrage, Replication, Cost of Carry

POW

No-arbitrage pricing is the comic law of the city.

Exam lens: Carry benefits, costs, storage, income, and financing connect spot and forward prices.
05

Forward Pricing and Valuation

FOCUS

Price at initiation and value after initiation are different panels.

Exam lens: Track underlying price, discounting, maturity, and cash flows.
06

Futures Pricing and Valuation

CHECK

Futures add daily settlement to the forward idea.

Exam lens: Margin, marking to market, convergence, and basis matter.
07

Swaps

BAM

A swap is a series of forward-like exchanges wearing one contract badge.

Exam lens: Fixed-for-floating, equity, currency, and interest-rate swaps depend on present value.
08

Options

ZOOM

Options draw asymmetric payoff shapes.

Exam lens: Intrinsic value, time value, moneyness, exercise style, and Greeks create intuition.
09

Put-Call Parity

AHA

Calls, puts, stock, and bonds can replicate each other.

Exam lens: Parity exposes mispricing and builds synthetic positions.
10

One-Period Binomial Model

POW

The tree splits into up and down worlds, then recombines into value.

Exam lens: Risk-neutral probability and backward induction make option pricing visible.
